Twelve Ways Out of Dm7

Everything here can replace Dm7 in a ii–V–I in C, arranged by how far it travels from the original. Each one is shown twice: a compact comping grip for when a bass player has the root covered, and a full grip with the root in the bass for solo playing. Dots are labelled with scale degrees, not fingers.

Before you commit to one

The upper-structure grips in the first band are safe under almost any melody — they only add notes to Dm7, they never contradict it. The rest rewrite the harmony: Dm7♭5 kills a melodic E, and the altered dominants clash with a held A. Check the melody note on beat one before you swap.

Everything transposes by sliding: this whole sheet in B♭ is the same shapes moved down two frets.
