Before you commit to one

The first band goes under any melody the tune already has — those voicings only add notes to G7. The alterations are pickier: G7♭9 kills a melodic A, G7alt fights a held D or E, and both sus voicings clash with a melody sitting on B.

The tritone subs need the bass player in on it, or at least not playing a G. If you are unsure, D♭7♯11 is the one to reach for — it contains the G, so it survives being contradicted from below.

Everything transposes by sliding: this whole sheet in F is the same shapes moved down two frets.
